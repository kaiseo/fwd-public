When does cancellation take effect?

Trace the interval between revoking permission and stopping a payment.

A worked example

A customer revokes an agent’s authority while a transfer is queued. The outcome depends on where the instruction has reached and which system receives the cancellation.

What to check

Put the customer action, bank acknowledgement, queue release, and settlement on one timeline. Identify which timestamp the customer can actually verify.
