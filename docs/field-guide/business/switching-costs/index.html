What would it take to switch?

Test how much of a workflow can travel to another vendor.

A worked example

A procurement team can export its documents, yet its approval rules, corrections, and evaluation examples may live inside a vendor’s product.

What to check

Choose a representative task and rebuild it from an export. Track missing fields, permissions that need recreating, and the time it takes to reproduce an accepted result.

Which part of the workflow has no usable export?
