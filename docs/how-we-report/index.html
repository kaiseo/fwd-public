How we report

How FWD examines the business decisions behind AI.

Start with a decision

Who gains the authority to act, and who carries the cost if something goes wrong? In our bank-run preview, the decision is whether a customer’s original permission should still let an agent move money when conditions change.

Trace the evidence

We look for the original filing, product documentation, or test results behind a claim. For a payments experiment, a useful record would show when the customer withdrew permission, when the bank received that instruction, and when the transfer settled.

Ask what the result supports

A sandbox can show how a control behaved under the conditions tested. To assess how it might work across a market, readers also need to know which participants took part, how long the test ran, and which conditions it left out.
